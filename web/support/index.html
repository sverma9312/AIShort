Open an issue on GitHub or email [contact email]. For a wrong or misleading summary, include the headline and the time you saw it.

Frequently asked questions

How are summaries written? Automatically, by AI models, from the original article. Each summary is checked against the article for numbers and names before it is published, but mistakes can happen — always check the original via "Read more".

How often is the news updated? Every 30 minutes.

How do I stop notifications? In the app, open Settings and turn off notifications, or disable them in your device settings.
