Support

Found a wrong summary, a broken link, or a bug? Open an issue at github.com/sverma9312/AIShort/issues and include the card's headline.

FAQ

How often is the news updated? About every 30 minutes.

Where do summaries come from? They are written automatically from the original article, which you can open with "Read more".

How do I stop notifications? In the app: Settings → Notifications, or in your phone's system settings.
