🤖 Android & Mobile Browser Tips:
  • Swipe Up / Down: Swipe vertically to flip smoothly between stories (cards snap into full view).
  • Tap to Zoom: Tap any card image to view in high resolution with pan and tap-to-zoom.
  • Run Full-Screen (No Address Bar): Tap ⋮ (three dots) in Chrome and tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen") to remove the browser address bar and run like a native app.
📱 iPhone Safari Tip:

Tap Share ⎋ at the bottom of Safari and select "Add to Home Screen" to run AIShort full-screen without address bars, just like a native iOS app.
