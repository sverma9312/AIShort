Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Template — review before publishing the app.

AIShort does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, settings and a cached copy of the news feed are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, an anonymous push token is sent to our database so we can send at most two breaking-news alerts a day. It is not linked to your identity. Turn notifications off in Settings to stop them; inactive tokens are deleted.

Crash reports and usage analytics

Unless you turn it off in the app's Settings, anonymous crash reports (Sentry) and usage statistics (PostHog), such as which screens are opened, are collected to fix bugs and improve the app. They contain no personal information.

News content

Summaries are generated from public news sources. Tapping "Read more" opens the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/sverma9312/AIShort/issues.
