Privacy policy

Effective [date]. Applies to the AIShort mobile app and this website.

Summary

AIShort has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, settings (theme, categories, notification and analytics preferences) and a cached copy of the news feed are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Push notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, your device's push token and its platform (iOS or Android) are stored in our database so we can send you up to two top-story alerts a day. The token does not identify you. Tokens that stop working, for example after you uninstall the app, are deleted automatically. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app's settings or your device settings.

Crash reports and usage analytics (optional)

To fix bugs and improve the app, it may send crash reports (via Sentry) and anonymous usage events such as "app opened" or "article opened" (via PostHog). These do not include your name, contacts or advertising identifier. You can turn this off in the app's settings.

News content and links

The app downloads the news feed from this website. Like any web server, GitHub Pages, which hosts it, may log technical data such as IP addresses. "Read more" opens the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Service providers

GitHub (website and feed hosting), Supabase (database for push tokens), Expo (delivering push notifications), Sentry (crash reports) and PostHog (analytics). They process data only to provide these services.

Children

AIShort is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Questions or requests: [contact email], or open an issue on GitHub.
