Our approach to model safety evaluations

Every model Example AI Lab releases goes through a structured set of safety evaluations before launch.

We test for dangerous capabilities in four areas: cybersecurity, chemical and biological risks, persuasion, and autonomous replication.

Starting this month, we will publish a summary of every evaluation within 30 days of a model launch, including tests the model failed.

External red teams from three universities now review our results, and their reports will be published alongside ours.
