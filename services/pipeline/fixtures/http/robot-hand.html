A robot hand that learns from video

Researchers at Example Robotics Lab have built a robot hand that learns new skills by watching short videos of people.

After watching ten examples, the hand learned to fold towels, open jars and sort screws, succeeding in 87 percent of trials.

Previous systems needed hundreds of demonstrations recorded with special gloves or motion-capture suits.

The team is releasing its training code and a dataset of 5,000 annotated videos so other labs can reproduce the results.
