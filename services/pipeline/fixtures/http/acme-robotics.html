Startup Acme Robotics raises $40 million to bring AI to warehouses

Acme Robotics, a startup that builds AI-powered picking robots for warehouses, has raised $40 million in a Series B round led by Example Ventures.

The company says its robots use a vision model to pick more than 10,000 different products without being reprogrammed, and they already work in 25 warehouses across Europe.

Acme plans to use the money to open an office in Austin and double its engineering team to 120 people by the end of next year.

Chief executive Dana Okafor said demand from retailers grew sharply this year as labor shortages continued.
